# Key Insights

- **Overall:** 1,000 unique orders report **1,145,049.83 sales units** and **199,687.65 profit units** for a **17.44% margin**. Currency is not specified consistently in the source.
- **Technology leads:** the category represents about **66.4% of sales**. Laptops are the top product by sales (365,347.19).
- **Regional opportunity:** North leads sales (243,949.83); West has the highest margin (20.01%). Central has the lowest margin (12.99%) despite 230,284.39 in sales.
- **Channel mix:** Online has the highest sales (390,936.01), but Retail Store has the highest profit (73,659.19) and margin (19.99%). Phone has the lowest margin (15.70%).
- **Year-over-year:** 2025 sales are down **10.44%** and profit down **28.67%** versus 2024; margin fell from **19.34% to 15.41%**.
- **Data caveats:** 12 repeated order IDs were removed; nine orders have no usable date; five negative-quantity orders all report positive sales. Validate these records and the profit calculation before making financial decisions.

## Suggested next steps

1. Reconcile duplicate IDs and negative quantity/sales records with the source system.
2. Investigate Central's lower margin and the 2025 profit decline by product, discount, and channel.
3. Review product and channel costs before interpreting margin differences as operational performance.
